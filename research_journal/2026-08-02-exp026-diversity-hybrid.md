# 2026-08-02 — EXP-026 diversity-preserving hybrid

## Stage and attribution

Registered synthetic confirmation; **Collaborative**. Richard asked Argus to keep testing the financial-genetics research problem after EXP-025. Argus specified and executed the diversity-preserving successor.

## Result

**NOT SUPPORTED.** The intervention successfully preserved behavioral diversity but failed every return/adaptation prediction. It significantly underperformed global random restart on the pooled full-year endpoint.

## Learning

“Preserve diversity” was too coarse. Evolutionary inheritance is valuable only when parental information remains relevant. After a large structural break, local descendants inherit obsolete coordinates. The next biological analogue is regulated mutation conditional on environmental distance—not a permanently fixed mutation/immigration mixture.

The result also distinguishes diversity as a possible risk regularizer from diversity as an alpha source. The hybrid often reduced volatility or expected shortfall relative to concentrated evolution, but that did not translate into superior net return.
