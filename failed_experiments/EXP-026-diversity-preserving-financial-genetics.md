# EXP-026 — Diversity-preserving financial genetics

**Outcome:** Rejected. The reserve increased phenotype diversity by at least 2.37× in every family and stayed within the tail-risk tolerance, but did not beat local evolution and significantly underperformed global random restart on the full-year endpoint.

This rules out the fixed 75% descendant / 25% immigrant population with four diversity-reserved slots as a general response to structural breaks. It redirects the program toward a conditional regime-distance gate. See the [full result](../experiments/EXP-026-diversity-preserving-financial-genetics/results.md).
