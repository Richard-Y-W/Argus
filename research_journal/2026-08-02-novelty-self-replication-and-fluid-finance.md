# 2026-08-02 — Novelty audit and a stricter cross-disciplinary program

Richard asked Argus to preserve the PLEX novelty assessment and broaden the biology/physics program to self-replication and fluid mechanics. The novelty audit found substantial adjacent work: virtual economies as laboratories, developer interventions, EVE inequality, post-launch PLEX summaries, hydrodynamic order books, reaction–diffusion markets, evolutionary finance, genetic-programmed trading, and market ecology are all established.

No located public paper or repository reconstructs the 2025 regional PLEX books, separates mechanical book cancellation and pooling from non-mechanical liquidity outcomes, and tests regional network adjustment against ordinary panel and time-series benchmarks. The exact event–archive–estimand combination therefore remains plausibly original, subject to a repeated search before any manuscript claim.

The fluid-mechanics idea was narrowed to an accounting question. Displayed order density can be placed over price coordinates, but a continuity equation closes only if arrivals, cancellations, modifications, and executions are observed or bounded. Full Navier–Stokes language would be premature. The first useful test is whether order-density changes can be reconciled with volume and source/sink terms, not whether a fluid-shaped model fits prices.

Self-replication was also split into economic and computational meanings. Capital allocation can reproduce successful strategies through replicator dynamics; code can reproduce through mutation and recombination. Both literatures already exist. For Argus, the novel discipline would be a complete genome/evaluation ledger, fixed evolutionary budget, equal-budget random-search baseline, and one untouched confirmation era. Otherwise mutation simply automates data snooping.

## Human growth digest

- **Concept:** a conservation law is stronger than an analogy because missing source and sink terms produce a measurable residual.
- **Mistake to avoid:** calling evolutionary optimization robust because many generations converge; selection can converge efficiently on noise.
- **Novelty lesson:** novelty can live in the event, archive, and estimand even when every individual method is established.
- **Best next question:** can the PLEX order-density accounting identity close well enough to justify a fluid-limit model?
