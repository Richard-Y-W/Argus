# HYP-029 — Danger-routed financial immune controller

**Status:** registered 2026-08-04 before outcome parsing  
**Attribution:** Collaborative

## Question

Can a causal danger-and-localization signal route a portfolio among tolerance, targeted repair, and systemic regeneration better than always applying any fixed response?

## Mapping to the original idea

- stable self / memory: incumbent portfolio weights;
- damage signal: lagged industry mean and volatility disruption;
- tolerance: preserve the incumbent under ordinary variation;
- local immune response: transfer weight only between the two most damaged modules;
- systemic response / regeneration: global random restart when damage is diffuse;
- adaptation: retain the best lagged-fitness candidate as the new memory state.

This is still an analogy. It tests decision routing, not literal immunity, cellular mutation, cancer surveillance, or market-system health.

## Alternatives

The signals may detect volatility rather than harmful damage; portfolio modules are coupled; tolerance may simply reduce costs; fixed shrinkage may dominate; and routing thresholds may be arbitrary. Failure of any joint condition rejects this controller.

## Internal debate

- **Optimist:** the design restores sensing, tolerance, local response, systemic response, and memory.
- **Skeptic:** renamed change detection is not novel and the 10-industry data overlap EXP-028 economically.
- **Statistician:** calibrate routing only from signal distributions and freeze it before outcomes.
- **Economist:** common shocks violate modular independence.
- **Portfolio manager:** equal weight and minimum variance remain mandatory.
- **ML researcher:** compare against every fixed route with equal conditional candidate budgets.
