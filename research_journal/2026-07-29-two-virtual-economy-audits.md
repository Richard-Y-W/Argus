# 2026-07-29 — Two audits narrow the virtual-economy research path

Argus completed two autonomous deep-research cycles after Richard requested continued work.

The first cycle built a point-in-time inventory of 21 conversion, transfer, and market-access events across EVE Online, World of Warcraft, Old School RuneScape, Steam/Counter-Strike, Rocket League, Roblox, and Second Life. The inventory did not reveal a public event that simultaneously has precise timing, a credible untreated comparison, adequate pre/post coverage, and an executable price. EVE's 2025 global PLEX market is the strongest direct market-integration event, while Roblox's 2026 age/geography-specific DevEx schedule is the strongest latent causal design if private creator data can be obtained.

The second cycle audited the developer-credibility hypothesis. It rejected a retrospective cross-platform credibility score because issuer identity, platform objectives, policy salience, and data quality would be confounded, and selecting “reversals” after viewing prices would leak outcomes into the treatment. The surviving research product is a prospective promise-ledger protocol. EVE has enough dated communications to pilot the measurement, but public spot prices do not reveal expectations or policy surprises.

Neither cycle registered `HYP-025` or `EXP-025`. That restraint follows the repository's promotion rule and avoids turning broad documentation into causal evidence. The next acquisition decision is narrow: test archival coverage for regional PLEX books and comparable EVE items around 2025-07-07, or seek creator/platform microdata for the Roblox DevEx cohort discontinuity.

## Human growth digest

- **Concept:** market access is multidimensional. An on-ramp, cash-out right, global order book, trade delay, and third-party resale route are different treatments.
- **Identification lesson:** policy credibility is an expectation held before a decision; it cannot be safely inferred from whether an intervention later “worked.”
- **Data lesson:** a long display-price history does not substitute for fills, depth, fees, treatment assignment, or a lawful liquidation route.
- **Optional question:** if you had one platform partnership, would you prioritize order-level PLEX integration data or Roblox creator-level DevEx data? The former has public-market continuity; the latter has cleaner cohort variation.
