# Evidence boundaries: synthetic mechanisms versus external market data

This table is the authoritative boundary for the current cross-domain program.

| Branch | Inputs | What has been verified | What may be claimed | What may not be claimed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXP-025–027 financial genetics | Deterministic synthetic multivariate AR(1) returns generated by code in each experiment | Seeds, regime parameters, causal timing, costs, equal search budgets, tests, result hashes | Behavior of the specified algorithms inside the registered simulators | Historical performance, alpha, investability, or that real markets possess DNA |
| EVE PLEX archive scout | EVE Ref market-order indexes and sampled snapshots; CCP notices | 2025-07-06 sampled snapshot had 2,003 PLEX orders across 59 regional books; 2025-07-08 sampled snapshot had 322 orders in global region 19000001; archive coverage exists | Feasibility of reconstructing the transition and provenance of sampled facts | A market-quality effect, causal welfare result, complete-panel accuracy, or USD investability |
| Future historical validation | Not yet assembled | Nothing yet | No empirical claim until point-in-time data, controls, costs, and untouched rolling-origin evaluation pass | Using current synthetic results as evidence of real-market profitability |

## Anti-overfitting rules

1. Every claim-bearing experiment is registered before outcome inspection.
2. Consumed seeds, families, dates, and thresholds cannot be reused for tuning and confirmation.
3. Candidate counts and search ledgers are matched across adaptive methods.
4. Failed predictions remain archived at full resolution.
5. Synthetic evidence must survive a new data-generating family before it can motivate, but never substitute for, historical validation.
6. Historical validation must use point-in-time observables, rolling-origin splits, realistic costs, untouched eras, and non-biological baselines.
7. PLEX archive acquisition requires pinned indexes, ETags or hashes, incomplete-snapshot rules, and transition-timestamp reconstruction before registration.

## Current verdict

The financial-genetics experiments are controlled computational probes. They have produced three rejected joint hypotheses and no real-market edge claim. The PLEX work is a verified data-feasibility and design branch, not a completed empirical study.
