# Digital assets and financial genetics: two connected but distinct programs

## Program map

### Digital-market topology

The EVE PLEX transition offers a concrete digital-asset question: what happens when 59 segmented regional order books become one global venue for an asset whose physical-delivery friction was already low?

The first empirical targets are non-mechanical:

- displayed spread and depth;
- concentration and imbalance;
- pre-pooling regional quote-gap diffusion;
- resilience after large book changes;
- transition timing, cancellations, and global-book formation.

The data source is the EVE Ref order-book archive, corroborated by CCP institutional notices. Only two sampled snapshots and archive availability have been verified so far. No effect estimate exists.

### Financial genetics

The synthetic branch defines strategy genomes, mutation, inheritance, phenotype, and selection. EXP-025–027 progressively rejected unconditional local mutation, a fixed diversity hybrid, and a generic environmental-distance gate.

The remaining scientific question is decision-aware inheritance value:

> Which observable changes make a particular strategy lineage obsolete, and can that be estimated without tuning to realized strategy outcomes?

This is not answered by generic market distance. It requires a mapping from environment changes through the genome's mechanism to its portfolio phenotype.

## Legitimate connection

The branches can connect only after the PLEX pipeline is complete. The PLEX topology shock could become an external case study of structural environmental change, while financial-genetics methods could provide hypotheses about memory versus reinitialization. One event cannot train or validate a general evolutionary controller.
