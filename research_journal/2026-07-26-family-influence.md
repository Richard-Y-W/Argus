# 2026-07-26 — Deleting a family nearly erases the mean

EXP-024 deleted each frozen family without reclustering. Removing the 59-factor cluster weakened the equal-factor contrast from -0.1535 to -0.0198 pp/month. The experiment rejected the no-family-dominance hypothesis. The family is empirically coherent but economically broad, so the result identifies concentration rather than a mechanism.

## Human growth digest
