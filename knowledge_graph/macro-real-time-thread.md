## Current boundaries

- Forecaster IDs are continuity proxies with documented identity ambiguity.
- RTDSM vintage dates establish a public-information cutoff, not the causal source or exact timing of each revision.
- Forecast accuracy, macro mechanism, yield response, and tradability remain separate claims.
