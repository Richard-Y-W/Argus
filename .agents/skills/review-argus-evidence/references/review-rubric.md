# Argus evidence-review rubric

## Design

- Falsifiable question and explicit alternatives
- Preregistered sample, endpoint, estimator, and decision rule
- Appropriate baseline, controls, ablations, and external validation
- Honest record of exploration and search breadth

## Data and inference

- Provenance, release, fingerprints, and transformation lineage
- Point-in-time availability, revisions, survivorship, missingness, filtering, joins, and duplicates
- Estimator matches the question and dependence is handled defensibly
- Multiple testing and specification search are disclosed
- Magnitudes, intervals, breadth, and sensitivity are reported

## Economic interpretation

- Mechanism is distinguished from correlation
- Return evidence is not treated as direct evidence of trading activity
- Gross predictability is not treated as net profitability
- Costs, capacity, liquidity, financing, turnover, and implementation remain bounded unless measured

## Engineering and communication

- Deterministic code, canonical loaders, tests, and reproducible commands
- Tables, figures, prose, and machine-readable outputs agree
- Failures and deviations remain visible
- Language does not exceed identification or external validity
