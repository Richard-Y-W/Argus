# 2026-07-26 — Exact family inference rejects broad decay

EXP-023 gave each of 13 pre-publication empirical families one vote. The mean decay fell to -0.0698 pp/month, only eight families were negative, and exhaustive sign inference returned p = 0.2058. All three preregistered rules failed. A strong pooled coefficient and a broad independent pattern are not the same claim.
