# 2026-08-02 — Digital-asset data gate opens; complex-systems methods narrowed

Richard asked Argus to revisit the digital-asset data and then proposed using ideas from biology and physics to search for a research or forecasting edge. The cycle did both, while treating “edge” as a claim that requires a benchmark rather than as a motivation.

The central empirical update is concrete. EVE Ref preserves twice-hourly full market snapshots around the July 2025 PLEX integration. A temporary two-file probe found 2,003 PLEX orders across 59 regions before integration and 322 orders in the new global region afterward. The files contain order IDs, sides, prices, remaining and original quantities, issue times, locations, and region identifiers. Daily history and CCP economic-report archives also exist. Raw files were not committed.

That evidence moves PLEX from an archive-acquisition question to an extraction-and-identification project. It does not itself show improved liquidity. Cross-region price dispersion disappears mechanically under a global book, the policy was announced, and there is no perfect untreated PLEX. The next cycle must identify the exact transition sequence and preregister non-mechanical market-quality outcomes.

The biology/physics review ranked spatial network diffusion first because regional topology and order-book states are observed. Order-book entropy is a secondary diagnostic if it beats simple concentration metrics. Hawkes models require a validated event tape; ecology requires capital or strategy abundance; critical slowing down has high crisis-selection and false-discovery risk. Those methods are not rejected, but their required observables are now explicit.

## Human growth digest

- **Concept:** a useful cross-disciplinary transfer maps state variables and mechanisms, not just vocabulary.
- **Mistake to avoid:** treating thousands of orders around one policy change as thousands of independent interventions.
- **Design lesson:** the obvious outcome—regional dispersion becoming zero—is mechanical and therefore the least interesting “result.”
- **Edge taxonomy:** scientific explanation, out-of-sample forecasting, and executable trading are three different claims.
- **Open question:** after displayed books become global, does depth replenish more reliably after shocks, or is liquidity merely relocated into one visible venue?
