The useful update is interpretive discipline. The archived US return association remains negative at the factor level, but breadth language must now be dependence-aware. Single-factor deletion stability is insufficient when many factors belong to one correlated construction family.

## Researcher digest

The lesson is the difference between "many rows" and "many independent reasons." A result can survive 141 one-factor deletions and still be mostly one family-level fact. In finance, dependence is often the denominator.
