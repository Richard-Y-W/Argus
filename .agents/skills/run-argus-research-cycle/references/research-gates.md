# Argus research gates

## Question to exploration

- The question is falsifiable and economically meaningful.
- At least one competing explanation is explicit.
- Data feasibility and likely identification limits are recorded.

## Exploration to registration

- Exploratory results are not presented as evidence.
- The estimand, sample, timing, transformations, controls, and decision rule are fixed.
- Leakage, survivorship, revisions, dependence, and multiple testing are addressed.
- A genuine untouched confirmation set or defensible registered design exists.

## Registration to claim

- The registration boundary predates confirmatory outcome inspection.
- Code and inputs are reproducible and provenance is recorded.
- Deviations are disclosed rather than rewritten away.
- Magnitude, uncertainty, robustness, and external validity are separated.
- Negative and inconclusive outcomes are archived at full resolution.

## Claim to practical relevance

- Direct evidence supports the proposed mechanism.
- Costs, turnover, capacity, liquidity, financing, and implementation timing are evaluated.
- The claim survives a credible external sample or data family.

Without these conditions, describe practical relevance as unresolved.
