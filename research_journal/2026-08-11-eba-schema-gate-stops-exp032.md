# 2026-08-11 — External schema improves realism but cannot identify intervention

Richard authorized the EBA continuation cycle after the immune-system review. Argus acquired the complete 2025 EBA stress-test public database, fingerprinted its five core files, and inspected more than 1.19 million rows covering 64 banks.

The release clears data lineage and solvency-endpoint gates. It does not clear the intervention gate. EBA publishes bank starting states and projected outcomes under common baseline and adverse scenarios, with a constrained static-balance-sheet assumption. It does not reveal what would happen if particular exposures were removed, and it contains no operational liquidity or funding endpoint. Targeted and proportional treatments would therefore be simulations authored by Argus rather than treatments tested by the release.

No HYP-032 or EXP-032 was registered. This is a useful negative cycle: it prevents an externally decorated but internally circular simulation. The branch is paused until an exogenous intervention design or independently validated response model exists.

Research stage: scouting and identification audit. Attribution: human-directed; Richard chose continuation, Argus selected and audited the EBA schema.
