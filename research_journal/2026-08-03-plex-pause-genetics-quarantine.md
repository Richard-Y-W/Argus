# 2026-08-03 — PLEX paused; genetics quarantined

Richard chose to pause the PLEX branch and prioritize financial genetics, explicitly warning against cross-contamination. Argus froze the verified PLEX archive state, marked its queued questions inactive, and created a code-level tripwire for genetics-owned machine artifacts.

The first candidate-data audit also changed the immediate plan. The local JKP global factor panel is authoritative and reproducible, but Argus has already examined its full 1986–2025 outcome history in EXP-013 and related work. It can support transparent exploratory genetics work, but not an untouched confirmation claim. The next registered genetics experiment therefore requires a separately sourced benchmark or a genuinely point-in-time boundary; the consumed EXP-025–027 simulator cannot be reopened for tuning.

Research stage: governance plus pre-outcome data triage. Attribution: collaborative. Richard made the branch and contamination-control decision; Argus formalized and enforced it.

## What changed in our understanding

Research branches contaminate one another through design choices as well as rows of data. A clean folder alone is insufficient: event windows, thresholds, metrics, and remembered outcomes can all leak across domains. The repository now records that broader boundary explicitly.
