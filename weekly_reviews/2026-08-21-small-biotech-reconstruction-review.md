# Adversarial review: small-biotech failure-reversal reconstruction

**Verdict:** INCONCLUSIVE; registration blocked  
**Claim reviewed:** the available data can support a survivorship-safe independent test of post-Phase-III-failure reversal

## Strongest evidence

Eight primary-source failure candidates have a validated return-blind schema. The two identity-matched free-price histories demonstrate that the event-window code path is technically feasible.

## Blocking findings

- Six of eight seed securities lack usable histories from the free source.
- Missingness is economically endogenous because these firms were acquired, merged, renamed, or disappeared.
- The published 92-event table and enough exact method detail are unavailable in the accessible article record.
- No event-time market-capitalization source exists locally, so “small” cannot be assigned without hindsight.
- All eight rows now have a single-coded market-session classification after a supplementary primary-source timestamp audit, but every classification and event still needs independent second coding.
- Delisting returns, financing overlap, cash runway, pipeline concentration, and borrow remain unidentified.

## Required wording

Call the work an independent reconstruction, not a replication, until sample equivalence is demonstrated. Call the eight rows candidates, not events in the analysis sample. Do not infer that missing free prices imply a loss or a reversal.
