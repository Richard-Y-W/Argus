# HYP-024 — No single empirical factor family carries US decay

*Registered 2026-07-26 before computing family assignments or outcomes. AI-led.*

Delete each of the 13 frozen EXP-022 clusters in turn and recompute the equal-factor mean post-publication-minus-in-sample contrast. P1: all 13 deletion estimates are negative. P2: the least-negative estimate is at most -0.10 percentage points per month. P3: the largest absolute shift from the full equal-factor mean is at most 0.03 percentage points per month. Survival requires all three. Any non-negative deletion estimate is a falsifier.

## Debate and limits

This is a deterministic influence audit, not repeated hypothesis testing. Cluster membership is empirical and may split or merge economic themes incorrectly. Stability rejects single-family dominance under this frozen partition only; it does not establish independent causal evidence. The Statistician forbids reclustering after deletion, and the Skeptic requires naming the most influential family and all members.
