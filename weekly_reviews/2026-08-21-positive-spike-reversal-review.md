# Adversarial review: positive-spike reversal smoke test

**Verdict:** REJECTED for the exact frozen sandbox rule  
**Promotion:** none; not claim eligible

## What failed

The mean, cost-adjusted mean, and breadth all pointed against the short thesis: -1.15% gross, -1.65% after 50 bp, and 40% positive across 10 eligible events. The interval is wide, but imprecision cannot rescue a wrong-signed realized mean under the frozen rule.

## Strongest opposing interpretation

Positive clinical announcements in these established firms may generate continuation rather than overreaction, or the close +1 entry may already exclude transient reversals. More importantly, the sample is too small and economically unlike the small, single-asset firms for which valuation overshoot and financing distress are most plausible.

## Blocking defects

- mutable, unlicensed price history;
- 10 eligible observations and repeated firms;
- no exact announcement times, halts, simultaneous news, or borrow data;
- SPY subtraction rather than a registered expected-return model;
- large-company seed, not the intended small-biotech universe; and
- no delisting or survivorship-safe security master.

## Anti-overfitting decision

Do not search alternative spike thresholds, entry days, or exits on this sample. Any future version must freeze those choices using a development period and evaluate them once on untouched events.
