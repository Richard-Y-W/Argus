# 2026-08-02 — Financial immune-system audit

## Result

**SUPPORTED WITH LIMITS.** Potential is moderate for adaptive portfolio/system risk management; broad novelty is low; narrow novelty is unresolved. No financial edge has been demonstrated.

## Strongest adversarial interpretation

The proposal relabels anomaly detection, hard constraints, continual learning, mixture-of-experts gating, policy pruning, and model-risk governance with biological names. The closest conceptual paper already proposes immune surveillance, adaptive response, memory, homeostasis, and a cancer analogy for finance. Classic AIS mechanisms also have known empirical limitations.

## What survived

The lineage-aware “cancer” component produces a potentially separable question: can a controller detect a component that remains locally profitable while increasing portfolio tail risk, liquidity demand, and hidden common ancestry? That can be falsified against an otherwise identical controller without lineage surveillance.

## Missing evidence

- No systematic-review-level search or patent audit.
- No benchmark implementation.
- No independent pathology labels in historical markets.
- No evidence that the full architecture improves risk-adjusted outcomes after costs.
- No evidence that biological mechanisms add value beyond generic controls.

## Decision

Do not promote to a preregistered empirical hypothesis. Add it to the queue as a synthetic methods candidate. The next action is an equal-budget pathology benchmark with an untouched pathology family and explicit rejection criteria.
