# EXP-028 results — Modular financial gene repair

## Classification

**NOT SUPPORTED.** Fault-localized mutation produced one positive primary sub-result but failed two of four registered joint conditions. It is not presently a credible financial edge.

## Evidence boundary

The official French December 2024 archive was downloaded and hashed before analysis. The hypothesis, sample, candidate budget, costs, endpoints, bootstrap, and decision rule were written before return parsing. Primary confirmation contains 120 months from 2015–2024. A current-file check contains 18 months from 2025 through June 2026 and cannot rescue the primary result.

This is a historical portfolio-control mechanism test. Industry research portfolios are not executable assets, and the result cannot identify net profitability, biological mechanism, capacity, or causal modularity.

Localized repair has a real-looking advantage over the two stochastic search controls in cumulative return, but it paid the highest primary volatility and tail loss. In the later-vintage check it still beat those search controls, while minimum variance and equal weight both delivered higher return with lower volatility. The simpler methods therefore weaken any practical case for evolutionary machinery.

## Adversarial review

**NOT SUPPORTED.** The narrowest supported statement is that this frozen localized operator achieved higher average primary certainty equivalent than unrestricted mutation, with an interval barely above zero. It did not establish incremental value over global restart, and the registered turnover mechanism went the wrong way.

Blocking findings:

1. The joint claim failed; the artifact cannot be promoted as a successful experiment.
2. Five industry weights are not independent biological modules. Common macro shocks and the budget constraint couple every phenotype.
3. The “damage” detector and candidate fitness reuse the same trailing data, so localization may simply be another noisy coordinate-search heuristic.
4. Conventional minimum variance dominated risk, while equal weight required no search or turnover. The comparison does not establish economic necessity.

Material limits: the bootstrap has one US time series and only ten annual blocks; later-vintage evidence has 18 months; stylized 10 bp turnover costs omit vehicles, slippage, taxes, and capacity; French returns may be revised. The precise primary interval is sensitive to the registered block design and should not be called robust replication.

## Bounded conclusion and next test

There is enough signal to justify one sharper mechanism test, but not enough to claim an edge. The interesting surviving question is whether localization helps only when shocks are genuinely sparse across independently defined modules. The minimum discriminating next experiment should stratify rebalances by a preregistered shock-sparsity score and test an interaction, ideally on a second geography or asset-family archive. It must not retune the mutation operator on these outcomes.
