# Small-biotech failure-reversal reconstruction gate — 2026-08-21

Richard authorized the next step after the large-firm positive-spike short failed. Argus traced the 2025 paper's accessible sample rules, audited source access, and began a return-blind independent reconstruction.

The paper's underlying 92-event table is not public in the accessible record. BioSpace's current news sitemap contains only 155 recent entries, and its robots policy disallows automated archive/search crawling. The larger Singh et al. dataset is also not a substitute: its clinical and return inputs require Citeline and CRSP subscriptions.

Argus therefore created an eight-event Phase III failure seed from primary SEC disclosures. The seed includes historical ticker, CIK, asset, trial, failure definition, announcement precision, source timing, and coding status. Every row remains single-coded and ineligible for returns.

A free-price coverage audit then requested a 252-session pre-period and 100-session post-period without calculating event returns. Only PRTA and historical AXON were covered and identity-matched. TORC, VTL, FOLD, IMGN, SAGE, and TTPH were absent. Excluding them would preferentially remove acquired, merged, or disappeared firms and make the reversal estimate survivorship-biased.

## Decision

Do not assign a hypothesis or experiment ID and do not calculate the two available returns. The minimum next input is a delisting-complete permanent-identifier return source, plus event-time shares/price or market capitalization. CRSP is the cleanest route; a licensed equivalent is acceptable if corporate actions and delisting returns are documented.
